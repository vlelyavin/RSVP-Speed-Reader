Quick Guide

Welcome to RSVP Speed Reader! This app helps you read faster by displaying one word at a time.

Getting started: Paste your text or upload a PDF file, then click Start to begin reading.

Adjust settings: Hover over the left edge to open reader settings where you can change font size, reading speed, and punctuation pauses.

View history: Hover over the right edge to access your saved documents and resume where you left off.

Keyboard shortcuts: Press Space to pause/play, R to reset, and arrow keys to step through words.
